Plain-language summary. Kavrelle is a demonstration store. You can browse, fill a cart and place orders, but no money changes hands and no goods are delivered. We keep only what the store needs to work.

1. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, zip code and the items you ordered.
  • Account details (only if you create an account): the same name, email and zip code, plus your password stored as a salted scrypt hash. We can never see your password.
  • Support chats: the messages you send to Kavrelle Support and the order actions taken.
  • Contact messages: your name, email, topic and message.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly in memory to rate-limit sign-in and contact attempts. It is not stored in our database.

2. How we use it

  • To place, show, track, cancel and refund your orders.
  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To send you service emails: order confirmations, cancellations, refunds, welcome and password-reset emails, and replies to contact messages. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To answer support chats and contact messages.
  • To measure and improve the reliability of the support assistant, using recorded test conversations and anonymous scores.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Cookies and local storage

We set one essential cookie, kv_session, only when you sign in. It is HttpOnly, limited to this site, and expires after 14 days or when you sign out.

Your browser’s local storage keeps your cart token, your chat conversation and your chosen view (3D or grid) so they survive a page reload. They never leave your device except when you use those features.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and load no third-party scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

4. The support assistant

Support chats are answered by an automated assistant that runs on our own server. Your messages are not sent to an outside AI service. Chats are stored so the conversation continues across reloads and so staff can review quality.

5. Who we share it with

Nobody, except: the email provider that delivers our service emails (when one is configured), and authorities where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Orders and contact messages: up to 24 months.
  • Sign-in sessions: 14 days. Password-reset links: 30 minutes, single use.
  • Support chats: up to 12 months.
  • Accounts: until you ask us to delete them.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Send the request through the Contact page with the topic “Privacy”, from the email address on your orders or account. We answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite, pages use a strict Content Security Policy, and sign-in and contact forms are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don’t use elsewhere.

9. Children

Kavrelle is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the effective date above changes too. Questions? Use the Contact page.
