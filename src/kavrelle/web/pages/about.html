About

A mall you can walk through.

Kavrelle puts twelve shops on two floors of a 3D building. Walk the concourse, step into a shop, pick something off the shelf. If 3D isn’t your thing, the grid view shows everything on one page.

12 shops, 2 floors

Fashion, sneakers, beauty, jewellery, electronics, home and more, each with its own shelves and photos.

Orders you control

Every order gets a short code. Track or cancel it any time with that code, your email and zip code.

Help that checks first

Kavrelle Support looks up your actual order before it answers, and follows the same rules as the website.

Why it exists

Kavrelle is a portfolio project. It shows a complete store, from the 3D front end to accounts, orders, emails and a support assistant, and it is used to measure how reliable an automated support assistant really is, with the 12 reliability metrics from the research paper Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability.

It is a demonstration: no payment is taken and nothing is shipped.

Built carefully

  • Strict security headers, hashed passwords and rate-limited sign-in.
  • No tracking, no ads, one essential cookie.
  • Tested end to end in a real browser on every change.
