About

A mall you can walk through.

Kavrelle puts twelve shops on two floors of a 3D building. Walk the concourse, step into a shop, pick something off the shelf. If 3D isn’t your thing, the grid view shows everything on one page.

12shops on two floors, each with its own shelves and photos
8characters in an order code you can track or cancel with
12reliability metrics the support assistant is measured on

Why it exists

Kavrelle is a portfolio project. It is a complete store, from the 3D front end to accounts, orders, emails and a support assistant. It is also a test bench: the assistant is measured with the 12 reliability metrics from the research paper Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability (Princeton, ICML 2026), the way a real company would check an assistant before letting it talk to customers.

Built carefully

  • Strict security headers, hashed passwords and rate-limited sign-in.
  • No tracking, no ads, one essential cookie.
  • Every feature is tested in a real browser on every change.

It is a demonstration: no payment is taken and nothing is shipped.

Created by

Sheraz Ashraf

Software engineering student · builds AI systems and the tools that test them

Sheraz designed and built Kavrelle end to end: the 3D mall, the shop and its accounts, the emails, the support assistant and the owner’s console. He started from a simple question: an AI assistant can look accurate in a demo, so how do you know it is reliable enough for real customers?

Kavrelle answers that question by putting an assistant in a working store and measuring it the way the research says to. It is part of a series of real-world AI projects he is building in the open.
