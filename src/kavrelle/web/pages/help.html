Help centre

How can we help?

Quick answers below. Still stuck? Send us a message or ask Kavrelle Support in the mall.

Shopping

How do I move around the 3D mall?

Drag to look around, use W and S (or the arrow keys) to walk, and click a shop to walk straight in. The directory on the left jumps to any shop. Press Esc to step back out.

I can’t see the 3D view.

Some devices can’t draw 3D. The site switches to the grid view automatically; you can also pick “Grid” at the top at any time.

Is anything actually charged or shipped?

No. Kavrelle is a demonstration store: orders are recorded so you can track and cancel them, but no payment is taken and nothing is shipped.

Orders

Where is my order code?

It appears right after checkout and in your confirmation email. Codes are 8 characters and never use easily-confused characters like 0/O or 1/I.

How do I track an order?

Click “Track order”, then enter the code with the email and zip code you used at checkout. Signed-in customers also see every order under their name.

Can I cancel?

Yes, while the order is pending. Open it in “Track order” and choose “Cancel order”. Shipped orders can’t be cancelled.

Shipping & returns

How long does shipping take?

In a real store this would be 3 to 5 working days. Here the dates are illustrative only.

How do returns and refunds work?

Refunds are recorded against the order and can never be more than the order cost. Because no payment is taken, no money actually moves.

Account & privacy

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can shop and track orders. An account just shows all your orders in one place. If you ordered as a guest, sign up with the same email and zip code to see those orders.

I forgot my password.

Use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in form, or go to the reset page. The link we email is valid for 30 minutes and works once.

What do you do with my data?

Only what the store needs. The Privacy Policy lists everything, in plain language.

Kavrelle Support

Who answers the chat?

An automated assistant that runs on our own server. It can look up and cancel orders after checking your code, email and zip code.

It got something wrong.

Sorry about that. Check the order with “Track order”, or write to us through the Contact page.
