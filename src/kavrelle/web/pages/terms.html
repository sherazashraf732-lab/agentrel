02

Demonstration store

Kavrelle exists to show how an online store works. Orders are recorded so you can track and cancel them, but no payment is collected and no product is shipped. Prices, stock levels and delivery dates shown on the site are illustrative.

Product photos are used under the Pexels license and remain the work of their photographers, who are credited on each product.

We may suspend accounts that break these Terms, try to harm the service or other users, or are used to send spam.

04

Orders, cancellations and refunds

When you place an order you receive an order code and a confirmation email. A pending order can be cancelled at any time from “Track order” using the code, email and zip code you used. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled.

Because no payment is taken, a refund simply records the amount against the order; no money is moved. Refunds can never exceed what an order cost.

05

Kavrelle Support chat

The support chat is answered by an automated assistant that runs on our own server. It can look up and cancel orders using the same checks as the website (order code, email and zip code). It can make mistakes; for anything important, use the order tools on the website or the Contact page.

Do not share passwords or payment card details in the chat. We never ask for them.
